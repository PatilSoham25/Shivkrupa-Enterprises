What's a Bus Stop Tensile?

A Bus Stop Tensile is basically a fabric roof held up by steel, made for places where people wait around. The fabric, usually PVC or HDPE, is stretched tight over a frame to make a roof that can handle the weather.
Unlike old-school concrete shelters, a Bus Stop Tensile is light, quick to put up, and looks way better. The stretched fabric spreads out the weight and lets rain run off easily. It's made to hold up against wind, sun, and all kinds of weather, so it lasts a long time outside.
