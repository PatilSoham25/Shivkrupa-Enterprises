What's a Membrane Tensile Structure?

A Membrane Tensile Structure is a light way to build stuff using strong fabric stretched over metal frames.
Instead of heavy concrete or roofs, it uses tension to stay up and keep its shape.

The fabric is usually polyester coated with PVC or something called PTFE.
It's made to handle sun, rain, and wind. The frame spreads out the tension so it stays strong and safe.

You see these a lot at stores, airports, malls, schools, stadiums, parking lots, and factories because they cover a lot of space without many posts in the way.
