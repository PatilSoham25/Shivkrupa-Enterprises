What's a Sun Shade?

A Sun Shade is like an umbrella for your stuff. It blocks sunlight and heat. You see them a lot over parking spaces, gardens, roofs, schools, restaurants, factories, and stores.
They're often made from tough fabric, plastic sheets, metal, things that hold up against the weather and sun.

Sun Shades are mostly for:

  • Cutting down on heat inside and out
  • Keeping cars from getting baked
  • Making shady spots to relax
  • Saving on energy bills
  • Making a place look nicer
