What's a Cone-Shaped Thing?

It's a roof made of fabric that's held up by steel. It looks like a cone. The fabric is super strong, like PVC or HDPE, and it's pulled tight over the steel to make it strong and keep the weather out.
For a Car Parking Shed, the cone shape spreads the weight evenly and lets the water run off. It's light but strong, so it's a good choice for places to park. It's faster to put up than those old concrete sheds, looks better, and you don't have to fix it much.
