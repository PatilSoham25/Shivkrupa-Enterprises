What's a Membrane Vault Tensile Structure?

A Membrane vault Tensile Structure is basically a fabric cover that's held up by a steel or aluminum frame attached to a wall.
Instead of using heavy stuff to keep it up, the fabric is stretched super tight across the frame. This makes it light but still really strong.

Vault usually means it's attached to a wall or side, so it covers the area above and a bit on the side.
That makes it perfect for entrances, hallways, fronts of buildings, and around the edges of places.

Because it's all about tension, it spreads weight out great, handles weather well, and looks modern.
You see these at malls, hotels, schools, office buildings, factories, and parks.
