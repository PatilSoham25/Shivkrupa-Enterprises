What's a Drop Awning, Anyway?

It's basically a vertical shade made of tough fabric or PVC. You stick it outside windows, balconies, patios, or open areas and roll it up or down when you need it. Unlike shades that stay put, you can tweak a drop awning to control sun, fresh air, and privacy super easily.
Usually, it's got a roller tube, some guides (like channels or wires), and strong outdoor fabric. You can crank it up and down or get a motor to do it for you.
You see these a lot on houses, restaurants, cafes, offices, and stores where keeping the sun and heat out is a must.
