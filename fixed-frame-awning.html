What's a Fixed Frame Awning?

A Fixed Frame Awning is a shading thing that stays put, held up by a strong frame, usually made of metal. Unlike those that roll up, these stay still and are stuck above doors, windows, or paths.
The material is pulled tight over the frame, making it look tidy. It's made to handle the weather and keep out sunlight, rain, and dust. because it doesn't move, these awnings are known to be strong and easy to care for.
You see these a lot on businesses, restaurants, hospitals, schools, and homes where they need shade and to keep the weather out.
