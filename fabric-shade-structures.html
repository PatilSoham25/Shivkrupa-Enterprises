What's a Fabric Shade?

A Fabric Shade is a structure made with special outdoor fabric that blocks sun, heat, and light rain.
You usually see them in open areas like parking lots, walkways, playgrounds, restaurants, resorts, warehouses, and home patios.

Unlike regular roofs, fabric shades are light but strong. They use fabrics that can handle sun, wind, and all sorts of weather.

Modern fabric shades come in shapes like:

  • Cantilever
  • Pyramid
  • Cone
  • Arches and walkways

They're useful and look modern too, so they work for businesses and homes.
