What's an Awning Shade?

Basically, it's a roof-like thing made of fabric, plastic, or metal that sits on a frame. You put it over doors, windows, or open areas to keep out sun, rain, and dust.
Unlike regular roofs, awnings can be any design you want – fixed, pull-out, or even with a motor. You see them on homes, restaurants, offices, shops, warehouses, everywhere.
The main idea is to make a cool, shady spot and keep the inside from getting too hot, avoiding sun damage.
