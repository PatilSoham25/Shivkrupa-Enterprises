What's a Folding Shade?

A Folding Shade is a covering that can be pulled back or folded up to keep out sunlight, rain, and dust.
Unlike roofs that stay put, this one can be opened and closed as you like. It's great for places where you want both open air and cover when you need it.

Usually, it's made of strong material, steel with a powder coating, aluminum, or PVC.
These shades are strong and easy to use. You often see them on patios, at cafes, in shopping centers, car parks, schools, and places where events happen.

People like folding shades 'cause they work well and look good. They make things comfier without blocking light or air all the time.
