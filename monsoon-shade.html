So, What's a Monsoon Shade?

A Monsoon Shade is a tough roof made of strong fabric or coated stuff that's held up by a strong frame.
It's specially designed to deal with monsoon weather heavy rain, humidity, and those crazy gusts of wind.

Unlike those cheap plastic tarps, a Monsoon Shade that's been designed by professionals will give you:

  • Protection that lasts for years
  • A design that drains water the right way
  • Protection from strong winds
  • Protection from the sun's rays
  • A nice, clean look

You'll mostly see these roofs on business properties, where keeping things dry is really important for business.
