What's a Retractable Awning?

A Retractable Awning is like a foldable shade made of fabric. It sits on a metal frame. Pull it out to get shade, and put it back when you don't need it. You can use a hand crank or a motor to move it.
They're usually made with strong fabric that can handle weather and block UV rays. The frame is aluminum or steel that won't rust.
Unlike those that stay put, you can move this around. This is a easy and energy-wise option for outside spots.
