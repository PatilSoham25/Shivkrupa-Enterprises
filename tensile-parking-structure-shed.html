What's a Cone-Shaped Tensile Structure?

It's a roof made of fabric that's held up by a pole in the middle and a steel frame, shaped like a cone. The fabric, usually PVC or HDPE, is pulled tight over the frame to make a roof that can handle weather.
For car parking, the cone shape helps spread the weight and lets rainwater slide off easily. The tight fabric stays put, even if the weather changes. These roofs are light, quick to put up, and can be made in lots of different designs, unlike normal concrete or metal sheds.
