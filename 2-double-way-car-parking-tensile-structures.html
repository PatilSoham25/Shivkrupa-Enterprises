What is a 2 Way Car Parking Tensile Anyway?

A 2 Way Car Parking Tensile is like a roof made of strong fabric that covers cars parked on both sides. It uses space better than those single-sided carports because it covers both sides with just one frame.
They're made with tough fabric like PVC or HDPE and held up with steel posts. The fabric stretches tight to make a strong, weatherproof roof. The way it's designed, water runs off easily, and it can handle windy conditions. It also looks pretty cool.
Large parking lots really like these because they cover a lot of cars and still look nice.
